Refund Policy

Wallet recharge and calling payment refunds

Last updated: July 2026. This Refund Policy explains how MakeCall handles wallet recharges, failed payments, duplicate payments and billed call charges for secure cloud calling and PSTN calling on makecall.in.

1. Wallet recharge payments

Wallet recharges are processed through Razorpay. When a payment is successful, the recharge amount is added to your MakeCall wallet and can be used for eligible voice calls on the platform.

2. Failed or pending payments

If your bank, UPI app, card issuer or payment provider debits an amount but MakeCall does not receive a successful payment confirmation, the payment may remain pending or fail. In such cases, the amount is typically reversed by the bank or payment provider according to their timelines. You may contact us with the payment reference if the amount is not reversed within 5–7 business days.

3. Duplicate payments

If the same recharge is charged more than once and only one wallet credit is provided, MakeCall will verify the Razorpay payment records and either credit the missing wallet amount or initiate a refund for the duplicate payment where applicable.

4. Non-refundable usage

Charges for calls that were successfully initiated, connected, routed, billed or otherwise consumed through MakeCall are non-refundable, except where required by applicable law or where MakeCall confirms a platform-side billing error.

5. Refund review process

To request a review, email us with your registered mobile number, recharge amount, payment date, Razorpay payment ID/order ID if available, and a brief explanation. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method whenever supported by the payment provider.

6. Refund timeline

Once approved, refunds are generally initiated within 5–7 business days. The actual credit date depends on the bank, card issuer, UPI provider or payment gateway settlement timeline.
