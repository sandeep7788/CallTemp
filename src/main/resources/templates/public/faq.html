Private, Unknown and Secret Calls: What You Should Know

People search for things like "unknown call", "secret call", "private calling" or "कॉल का पता न चले" for very different reasons — some want to stop their personal number from being shared, some are curious about hidden-number services, and some are asking whether an app can make a call look completely untraceable. These are not all the same thing, so here is what each term actually means and what MakeCall does:

  • Private calling usually means the person you call doesn't see your personal mobile number. MakeCall does this by design — every call is routed through MakeCall's own virtual number, not your SIM.
  • Unknown caller ID / hidden number call usually means the recipient's phone shows "Unknown" or no number at all. MakeCall does not do this — the recipient always sees a real, valid virtual number.
  • Secret or anonymous calling usually implies the call can't be traced back to the caller. MakeCall requires sign-in and keeps call records for billing and support, so calls are never anonymous or untraceable.
  • Internet / browser calling is simply what MakeCall is: a web dialer that places real voice calls to mobile and landline (PSTN) numbers over the internet, from your browser, without an app.

In short: MakeCall gives you genuine number privacy (your personal number is never revealed), but it is not an anonymous-calling, number-spoofing or prank-calling service.

General Questions

How do I make online calls with MakeCall?

Sign in to MakeCall.in, add credit to your wallet, enter the phone number on the dial pad and tap Call. You can call mobile and landline (PSTN) numbers in India directly from your browser — no app download needed.

What is the MakeCall calling rate?

MakeCall charges ₹6 per minute for calls to mobile and landline numbers, billed per second from the moment your call connects.

Can I call landline numbers with MakeCall?

Yes. MakeCall supports both mobile and landline (PSTN) numbers in India — enter the number with its STD code and tap Call.

Do I need to download an app to use MakeCall?

No. MakeCall works entirely inside your web browser using WebRTC technology. Just visit makecall.in, sign in, and start calling — no app install required.

Number Privacy, Unknown Calls & Prank Calls

Can I make an unknown, private or secret call with MakeCall?

MakeCall is an online calling platform for making regular voice calls to mobile and landline numbers — it is not an anonymous or secret-calling service. When you call someone through MakeCall, the person you're calling sees a MakeCall-assigned virtual number, not your personal mobile number. That gives you a genuine layer of number privacy, but you cannot make the call appear as "Unknown", blank or untraceable, and you cannot choose or spoof a custom caller ID.

Does MakeCall hide or mask my personal phone number from the person I call?

Yes, in the sense that your personal mobile number is never shown to the person you call — calls are routed through MakeCall's own virtual number, not your SIM number. This is different from a "hidden number" or "unknown caller" service: the recipient still sees a real, valid number on their phone, just not yours.

Can I use MakeCall to make anonymous calls that cannot be traced back to me?

No. MakeCall requires sign-in and links every call to your account for billing, and call records are retained as described in our Privacy Policy. It is not designed for anonymous or untraceable calling.

Can I use MakeCall for prank calls?

No. MakeCall's Terms & Conditions explicitly prohibit prank, hoax, harassing or misleading calls. MakeCall is intended for genuine personal and business communication, not entertainment-style prank calling.
